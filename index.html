About me

Hi there👋 I am Zihan Fang (房梓晗). I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Vanderbilt University, guided by Dr. Yu Huang. I earned my Bachelor's degree in Information Management from Soochow University, and my Master's degree in Data Science from Vanderbilt University. My research develops human-centered AI that lowers barriers to expertise and participation in software engineering. I combine large-scale repository mining, cognitive and behavioral modeling, and reinforcement learning to understand how people develop expertise and translate these insights into AI-supported programming systems and immersive learning environments, with particular attention to novices. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in my work!
